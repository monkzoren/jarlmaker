## Goal
The 3.4 tick budget becomes a test: a helper builds a synthetic 200-player world in `MemoryStore`, and each system's `budget.test.ts` asserts its tick time.

## Definition of done
- [ ] `buildSyntheticWorld({players: 200, seed})` and `expectTickBudget(system, ms)` helpers
- [ ] `entity/budget.test.ts` asserts the entity tick for 200 moving players at 10 Hz is within its share (document the share table in `tick.ts`)
- [ ] Budget tests are deterministic enough for CI (median of N runs, generous CI factor documented)
- [ ] `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` green locally

## Notes
The total budget is ≤ 2 ms per tick for 200 players at 10 Hz. Give each system a share, and write the share table next to the tick order.
