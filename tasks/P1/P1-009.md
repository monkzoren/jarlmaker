## Goal
A seed renders to PNG for review and for golden tests. Generator drift fails CI unless `WORLDGEN_VER` moved with a decision record.

## Definition of done
- [ ] `pnpm worldview --seed N --zoom z --out f.png` renders biome colours plus features and POI markers
- [ ] `pnpm worldgold` compares 5 seeds × 3 zooms to committed PNGs (exact match) and fails with a diff image path
- [ ] Goldens are keyed by `WORLDGEN_VER`; a mismatch with an unchanged version fails, and a moved version requires regenerated goldens
- [ ] CI `worldgold` step replaces the placeholder
- [ ] `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` green locally

## Notes
Keep goldens small (≤ 256×256) to stay kind to git.
