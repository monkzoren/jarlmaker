## Goal
A minimap drawn from the pure biome generator at low resolution, masked by explored sectors, with a compass pip pointing at the nearest POI of a selected class.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Minimap texture regenerated only when the player changes sector
- [ ] Unexplored sectors hidden; the player dot shows facing
- [ ] Compass pip uses `nearestPoi`; the class is selectable (defaults to wreck)
- [ ] Touch-friendly: tap expands, tap again collapses
- [ ] `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` green locally
