## Goal
You wash up on a beach beside the wreck of your longship. Open grey sea lies to the west, surf breaks on the sand, and the land climbs east through meadow, pinewood and heather moor to the fells. Pines and rocks stand in the world and you walk behind them. You can't walk into the sea, a tree or the wreck, on the server or in prediction. The camera follows you, and terrain streams in chunk by chunk as you explore. Everything comes from one seed with the same pure rules on every host, so no terrain goes over the wire.
