## Goal
A pixel-perfect camera: it follows the predicted local player, uses integer zoom only, rounds positions at draw time (never in the sim), and handles resize and orientation changes.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Zoom 2×/3×/4× chosen from DPI and viewport; user can step zoom within that set
- [ ] Positions rounded at draw only; a unit test proves sim positions stay fractional
- [ ] No shimmer when walking diagonally (screen capture or GIF in PR)
- [ ] `pnpm build`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` green locally
