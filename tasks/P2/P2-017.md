## Goal
The first survival loop. Face a pine and press Space (or the axe button): each swing takes a chip, and three swings topple it for three wood, leaving a stump you can walk over. Rocks break for stone. An inventory bar counts what you carry, and "+1 Wood" rises from your Viking. Press B (or the campfire button) and a ghost campfire shows where it will go. Space places it for 5 wood and 3 stone: it crackles, glows, and blocks the cell. Every hit and build runs on the server and reaches every player, and trees you fell stay felled for everyone.
