## Goal
The first night. A day lasts 6 minutes. About 4 minutes in, "Night 1 is coming" appears, the light fades, and the world goes dark except around campfires and a small light you carry. Three meters show health, warmth and food. The night drains warmth; a campfire's light warms you back. At zero warmth you lose health, and if the cold takes you, you wake on the beach by the wreck. Juniper bushes give berries; F (or tapping them) eats. Food drains slowly and never kills, but hungry you don't heal. A castaway who builds a fire comes through the night unhurt; one who doesn't barely survives night 1 and dies in night 2. That is the spec's own test.
